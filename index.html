What is LiveLocal?

LiveLocal is a platform designed to help music fans easily discover concerts happening near them. Instead of searching multiple websites, users can explore upcoming shows, discover new artists, and quickly find events happening in their city.

Our goal is to make it easier for concert-goers to stay connected to the live music scene. Artists and venues can promote upcoming performances, while fans can browse events in one place. Each listing provides key details such as the artist, date, venue, and location, along with direct links to trusted ticket platforms like Ticketmaster or StubHub. By bringing this information together, LiveLocal helps fans find great shows and supports artists performing in local communities.
